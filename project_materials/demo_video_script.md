# Spectrum — 2-minute demo video script

**0:00–0:15 | The problem**  
**Show:** Title card, then a teen looking at a phone with a blurred chat.  
**Say:** “A lot of safety tools watch, block, or report teens. That can make young people hide what’s happening. Spectrum takes a different approach: it helps a teen understand a conversation and choose what support they want.”

**0:15–0:30 | The product promise**  
**Show:** Spectrum home screen and its note that the teen controls what they paste and share.  
**Say:** “Spectrum doesn’t monitor an account. The teen chooses a conversation, can remove names, and asks for a check-in. The result is guidance, not a verdict.”

**0:30–1:00 | Grooming pattern demo**  
**Show:** Load “Grooming: secrecy and isolation,” then click “Check this conversation.” Highlight the secrecy request and request for school and route details.  
**Say:** “Here, Spectrum points to specific messages: ‘Don’t tell your parents’ and a request for identifying details. It explains why secrecy and isolation can matter, without blaming the teen. It also offers a boundary they could use.”

**1:00–1:20 | Cyberbullying demo**  
**Show:** Load the cyberbullying example and run the check. Highlight the threat to publish an embarrassing photo.  
**Say:** “In this example, the signal is a threat used to pressure someone. Spectrum names the behavior and gives the teen options for support. It doesn’t ask for or display any image.”

**1:20–1:35 | Avoiding overreaction**  
**Show:** Load the benign library-plans example and run the check.  
**Say:** “A safety tool also needs restraint. This ordinary exchange should come back with no clear high-risk pattern. The model is instructed to use evidence and avoid treating normal friendliness as a red flag.”

**1:35–1:50 | Teen-controlled escalation**  
**Show:** Click “Prepare a shareable summary.” Review the editable draft; do not send it.  
**Say:** “If the teen wants help, they can prepare a summary for a trusted adult. They review it first. Spectrum never sends anything automatically.”

**1:50–2:00 | Close**  
**Show:** Learn More section, then the Spectrum title.  
**Say:** “Spectrum turns AI pattern detection into digital literacy and a path to support—while leaving the choice with the teen.”
